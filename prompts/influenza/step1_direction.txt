IMPORTANT — the NO-CHANGE band (read carefully; it is RELATIVE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The baseline is already a strong forecast. "no change" does NOT mean the change is exactly
zero — it means the warranted change is SMALL RELATIVE TO THE BASELINE. There is a
proportional tolerance band of ±{nochange_band} of the baseline value around each period's
baseline:

  • If the change you would make is within ±{nochange_band} of that period's baseline value
    (e.g. a move of 8 on a baseline of 100), you MUST call it "no change" — treat the baseline
    as already accurate and keep it unchanged.
  • Only call "increase" or "decrease" when the evidence points to a change CLEARLY LARGER
    than ±{nochange_band} of the baseline value for that period.
  • The band is proportional: the same absolute move can be "no change" at a high level but a
    real "increase"/"decrease" at a low one. Always compare the move to the baseline level of
    THAT period.
  • Do NOT turn weak, vague, or ambiguous text into a direction. When the report text gives no
    clear up/down signal, the correct answer is "no change".

How to reason (one flowing paragraph, then the final line)
